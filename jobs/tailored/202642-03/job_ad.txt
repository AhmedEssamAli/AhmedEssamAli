The Opportunity

The Director, Customer Service functions in a critical senior leadership role responsible for customer experience strategy, service operations excellence, and digital transformation across customer support channels at an area level. This leader will drive the adoption of AI-enabled service capabilities, automation, analytics, and self-service solutions to enhance customer outcomes, increase operational efficiency, and support business growth while overseeing contact center operations handling millions of customer interactions annually. This role is with Abbott Diabetes Care (ADC) based at our divisional headquarters in Alameda, CA.

Work You’ll Work On

Oversee all aspects of customer support operations, including but not limited to, contact management, complaint handling, training and service quality, supplier management, warranty and return logistics, technical support, customer implementation and launch support for all ADC products and services.

Ensure that contact center operations are aided in their mission to provide a differentiated and exceptional customer experience in compliance with Abbott’s Quality System.
Ensure that Area Customer Service and BPO vendor partner(s) meet and exceed business goals by directing an organization of both in-house and outsourced partner vendors.
Provide leadership across a broad range of Customer Service, Business Process Outsourcers (BPOs) and Technical Service teams and are responsible for a multi-million-dollar budget.
Attract, lead and develop talent at group level to deliver excellence through highly effective coaching and leadership.
Act as a change agent in the organization by influencing leadership as to the importance of the service organization and acceptance of the customer experience service strategy.
Manage BPO performance with accountability for key conversational metrics including SLAs, AHT, CSAT, NPS, quality, cost to serve and similar performance indicators.
Operating as liaison between Abbott’s Diabetes Care home office functions (Marketing, Finance, HR, Legal, Quality, R&D, and Field Sales, IT) and Senior Leadership as it pertains to service, voice of customer, product launches, digital service experience, support needs, technical issues and special projects for support program development and implementation.
Oversee implementing and maintaining the effectiveness of the quality system. Specifically, oversee the management of the complaint intake portion of the quality system for ADC, ensuring integrity of records, operations to policies and procedures.
Communicate with Quality and Regulatory Affairs, Global Strategic Marketing, and R&D to ensure a positive impact on customer satisfaction, future sales and product improvements.
Measure customer satisfaction and voice of customer on an ongoing basis and work as part of the Commercial team to foster continuous improvement of service and customer experience.

This position may travel both domestically and internationally up to 20% of the time.

Required Qualifications

Bachelor's Degree in Life Sciences, Engineering or related technical discipline
Minimum 10 years in any combination of customer service/complaint handling management, implementation management, service operations management, program or project management and consulting
Previous strategic planning, project and people management
Ability to easily manage and be flexible to fluctuations in demand of work and growth; effectively manage by reporting and strategizing on resource needs
Demonstrated experience leading AI-enabled customer service transformation initiatives including conversational AI, agent-assist technologies, speech and text analytics, workflow automation, customer intent modeling, and self-service solutions

Preferred Qualifications

Post Graduate work in Business or Healthcare
In-depth expertise in contact center forecasting, scheduling and capacity planning
Strong working knowledge of omni-channel operations (e.g., voice, text, social)
Significant experience managing high volume outsourced contact centers
Experience with cloud-based telephony systems, speech analytics, and Salesforce
Experience defining and executing customer service technology roadmap, including AI platforms, CRM systems, automation solutions, analytics tools, and digital engagement technologies
