Why Deliveroo?

Our mission is to transform the way people shop and eat, connecting consumers, restaurants, shops, and riders to bring the neighbourhood to their door. We're redefining convenience by giving customers the ability to buy what they want, as they want it, when and where they want it.

Deliveroo is a technology-driven company at the forefront of one of the world's most dynamic industries. We are a relatively small team making a very large impact, moving fast, valuing ownership, and tackling some of the most interesting challenges in global commerce. We're driven by innovation, autonomy, and the ambition to deliver exceptional experiences for our customers, partners, and riders alike.

What You'll Be Doing

You will own frontline operational performance across two closely linked parts of Deliveroo's customer support network: an in-house Global Escalations team of around 50 agents based across the UK and Georgia, and the outsourced network delivering support on our behalf through three BPO partners and three Shared Service Centres, circa 1,500 agents across multiple locations, including the team leads and ops managers embedded within those outsourced functions.

Global Escalations sits at the heart of Deliveroo's most critical customer moments. The team manages our most sensitive, high-risk, and high-impact contacts, including complaints escalated to the Executive team. As the leader directly accountable for this recovery-focused centre of excellence, you will ensure the team operates with exceptional skill, judgement, and empathy, protecting Deliveroo's reputation, driving improvement through root cause analysis, and embedding a culture of integrity and customer-first recovery across the organisation.

For the outsourced network, you own how performance is delivered day to day, working closely with the team leads and ops managers embedded in each BPO and Shared Service Centre, not just the vendor relationship at arm's length. You own the operational rigour needed to make the network the best in class, from an experience and consistency perspective, across every market it supports.

Your key objective is to drive qualitative improvements to the customer experience, measured through CSAT, while balancing cost metrics like average handling time (AHT) and refund costs, rather than trading one off against the other. You will be the voice of Support to Deliveroo's market General Managers, taking full accountability for the Support experience in your markets, and you will partner closely with cross-functional Support & Operations teams, including Workforce Management, Training, Quality, and Policy, to drive initiatives that positively impact all three sides of the marketplace: customers, riders, and merchants.
