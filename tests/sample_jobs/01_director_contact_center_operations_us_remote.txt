Director of Contact Center Operations (US Remote)

About the role
We are looking for a Director of Contact Center Operations to lead a multi-site customer service operation supporting healthcare and financial services clients across the United States. This is a fully remote role with occasional travel for client business reviews.

What you will do
You will own the end-to-end operating model for more than 1,500 agents across omnichannel voice, chat and email. You will set and manage SLA and KPI targets, own the operating budget and forecasting process, and deliver the P&L for your client portfolio. Workforce management and capacity planning will sit with you, and you will drive attrition reduction and employee engagement through a clear coaching and development model.

You will partner with client leadership on quality programs, continuous improvement and root cause analysis. Our COPC-aligned quality framework is a core part of how we work, and Lean Six Sigma experience is valued. You will use Power BI dashboards and SQL-based reporting to give leaders real-time visibility into CSAT, NPS and first contact resolution.

Expectations
You will lead Team Leaders and Contact Center Managers, drive stakeholder management across our client and internal teams, and sponsor automation and digital transformation initiatives, including self-service and generative AI pilots. You will also manage vendor relationships and escalations.

What we are looking for
At least ten years in BPO or contact center leadership, including P&L accountability. Proven record of client relationship management and account management in a multi-client environment. Strong data skills, a track record of change management, and clear communication with executives.
