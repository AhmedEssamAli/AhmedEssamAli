Head of Customer Experience (Dubai, UAE)

About the role
We are hiring a Head of Customer Experience to define and deliver the customer experience strategy for our regional service centers in Dubai. You will report to the Chief Operating Officer and work closely with commercial, technology and client teams.

What you will do
You will set the CX vision and roadmap, owning customer satisfaction, NPS and CSAT targets across voice, digital and messaging channels. You will lead the voice of customer programme, using analytics and dashboards to turn feedback into process improvement. You will drive digital transformation and automation, including chatbot and self-service journeys, and evaluate generative AI use cases for agent assist.

You will oversee quality assurance, service level management and the KPI framework for all client programmes. You will manage the CX budget, forecasting and capacity planning, and be accountable for the P&L of the CX portfolio.

Who you are
You have 12 or more years in customer service or BPO operations, with at least five years in a senior leadership role. You have led change management at scale, built strong stakeholder management with executives, and managed vendor management and outsourcing partners. Experience with SQL, Power BI or similar reporting tools is expected. Lean Six Sigma or COPC certification is an advantage. Arabic and English are required.

What we offer
A competitive package, relocation support, and a leadership team committed to operational excellence and continuous improvement.
