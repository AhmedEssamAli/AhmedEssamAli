Client Services Director, BPO (Toronto or Remote, Canada)

About the role
A growing business process outsourcing company in Canada is seeking a Client Services Director to lead a portfolio of enterprise clients in telecommunications, retail and government services. You will be the senior executive accountable for client satisfaction, delivery and growth.

What you will do
You will own client relationship management and account management for the portfolio, running executive business reviews and quarterly business reviews with client sponsors. You will manage SLA and KPI performance, drive quality improvement, and lead escalations to resolution.

You are accountable for the P&L of the client portfolio, including revenue, budget, forecasting and margin. You will lead workforce management and capacity planning across contact center sites in Canada and offshore delivery partners, and manage vendor management relationships.

You will champion process improvement and continuous improvement using Lean Six Sigma and COPC standards. You will sponsor automation and digital transformation projects, including omnichannel service design and self-service tools. Reporting uses Power BI and SQL, and you will present insights to executive stakeholders.

Requirements
Fifteen years of experience in BPO or customer operations, with at least five years in director-level roles. Proven contract negotiation and business development experience. Strong stakeholder management and change management capability. Familiarity with CSAT, NPS and attrition management. Bilingual English and French is preferred.
