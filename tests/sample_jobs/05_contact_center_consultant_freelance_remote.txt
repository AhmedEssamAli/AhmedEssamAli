Contact Center Consultant (Freelance, Remote)

About the engagement
A mid-sized outsourcing firm is looking for an experienced freelance Contact Center Consultant to support a six-month operational improvement engagement. The work is fully remote, with flexible hours and a mix of client calls and written deliverables.

What you will do
You will review the client's contact center operations and produce a clear diagnosis of performance gaps. Your focus areas will be SLA and KPI performance, first contact resolution, average handle time and attrition. You will build a forecasting and capacity model, review workforce management practices, and recommend changes to the staffing plan.

You will assess the quality assurance programme against COPC standards and recommend process improvement actions. Where useful, you will propose automation or self-service options and estimate their operational impact.

You will prepare a budget view and a P&L impact summary for leadership, and build KPI dashboards in Power BI or Excel. SQL skills are helpful for pulling data from the client's CRM.

You will run stakeholder management sessions with operations managers and present findings to the executive sponsor. Change management support for team leaders will be part of the implementation phase.

What we need
Ten or more years of contact center or BPO operations experience, with consulting or advisory exposure. Proven record of delivering operational improvement, ideally with Lean Six Sigma certification. Strong communication, clear written reporting and the ability to work independently. Please send your day rate, availability and two client references.
